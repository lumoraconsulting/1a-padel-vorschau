FAQ

Questions.

Anything else?

Drop us a line, we reply quickly.

Contactinfo@1a-padel.com
When does 1A PADEL open?

We're planning to open in the fourth quarter of 2026. We'll share the exact date first with everyone on our list. Sign up to the newsletter and you'll be among the first to know.

Where do I find the arena?

Julius-Bamberger-Straße 5, 28279 Bremen, in the Habenhausen commercial area, just a few minutes from the A1.

How many courts will there be?

Eleven courts: nine indoor (eight doubles and one singles court) and two outdoor. That makes 1A PADEL the biggest padel hall in Bremen. We prefer to call it an arena.

Can I book or become a member yet?

Not yet: booking and memberships are in preparation. Everyone on the list gets to reserve first as soon as we go live.

I've never played padel. Is it for me?

Absolutely. Padel takes minutes to learn and is fun from the very first rally. You don't need to play tennis or bring any experience. If you want to play padel in Bremen, just come by.

Do I need my own equipment?

No. You can rent rackets and balls from us on site. Just bring sports shoes, we'll take care of the rest.

Are there tournaments and events?

Yes, planned from day one. Tournaments and leagues for every level, plus company and team events. 1A PADEL is meant to be a meeting point, not just a place to play.

What about parking?

There's dedicated parking right at the arena.

When is the arena open?

Around the clock, 24/7. Booking and door access run automatically via app and door code, so you can play early in the morning just as easily as late at night. It all starts with our opening in Q4 2026.

How do I stay up to date?

Best via our newsletter. You’ll be the first to see the arena take shape piece by piece, and you’ll hear every date before it goes public. Got a question? Email us anytime at <a href="mailto:info@1a-padel.com">info@1a-padel.com</a>.

What is padel and how is it different from tennis?

Padel is a mix of tennis and squash: you mostly play doubles on a smaller court with glass walls that you can play off, squash-style. The rackets are shorter and serves are underhand, which makes padel much faster to learn than tennis, and fun from the very first rally.

How many players do you need for padel?

Padel is classically played as doubles, two against two. On our single court you can also go one on one. Coming alone? You’ll quickly find partners in the community and at open matches.

How much does playing at 1A PADEL cost?

We’ll publish prices for court rental and memberships in good time before opening. Newsletter subscribers hear first, including exclusive priority booking.

Is there coaching for beginners, kids and juniors?

Yes. Our academy launches with programmes for every level: from taster sessions for beginners to kids’ and youth training through to tactics coaching for competitive players.

Are there changing rooms and showers?

Yes, modern changing rooms, showers and lockers are part of the concept, plus a lounge and food & drinks for after the match.
